The Partner API lets you send leads to TatvaCare and read them back. You can also add activities, calls, notes, files, and support tickets.

Your API key decides what you can do. It sets the fields you can send and read, and the product line and group of TatvaCare that your leads belong to. **There is no fixed payload.** Call [`lead_schema`](/discover-schema) to get the fields for your key, and build your requests from its response. Don't hardcode a field list.

## What you can work with

You use one key for all of these records.

| Record | What it is | Operations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | A person's record, with details such as lab results and plan | schema, get, list, create, update, delete |
| Activity | A form filled in against a lead for one task type | schema, get, list, create, update, delete |
| Call | A call logged against a lead | schema, get, list, create, update, delete |
| Note | A text note on a lead | schema, get, list, create, update, delete |
| File | An attachment on a lead, scanned for malware before it's stored | schema, get, list, attach, delete |
| Deal | The sale the CRM records when a lead converts | get, update |
| Ticket | A support ticket in your product line, which can link to a lead | schema, get, list, create, update, delete |
| Ticket comment | A comment on a ticket | schema, get, list, create, update, delete |

Files have no update. To replace a file, attach the new one and delete the old one. Deals have no create or delete, because the CRM creates the deal itself.

Every record except deals also has bulk operations that handle many records in one call. For very large loads, submit a [bulk job](/async-bulk) and check its status until it finishes.

Each operation in the [API Reference](/partner-api) has a playground where you can send a real request.

## How leads work

> Your key, issued by TatvaCare, sets the product line and group of every lead. You never send them. Send your lead data as it is.

The CRM identifies a lead by its phone number (`mobile_no`) plus the product line and group of your key.

If you send a lead with a phone number the CRM already has, the CRM updates that lead. It doesn't create a second one. This means you can safely send the same lead again. You don't need to check whether it exists first. The [Quickstart](/partner-quickstart) shows the recommended order of calls.

Each lead has a **unique ID** in the `name` field, for example `a7f3c9e12b`. Store it, because you need it to read, update, or delete the lead. You can also read a lead by its phone number.

The program (`custom_current_program`) isn't part of a lead's identity. If a lead moves to a new program, it stays the same lead. Depending on your key, you send the program, your key sets it, or leads have no program. `lead_schema` tells you which one applies. If you send the program, it also lists the programs you can use.

A lead also has sections for extra details, such as lab results, plan details, and the care team. Send each section as a JSON array inside the lead. You create and update sections with the same lead call.

Other records work differently. If you send the same create for an activity, call, note, file, or ticket twice, the CRM creates two records. To retry those safely, send an `Idempotency-Key` header. See [Writes and retries](/writes-and-retries).

For the full rules, see [Lead identity](/concepts).
